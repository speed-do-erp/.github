# SPEED-DO

ERP for organizations of every size, from a single company to a university, built on [Frappe Framework](https://frappe.io/framework) and [ERPNext](https://erpnext.com) v16, with Saudi Arabia support and a university system.

- **Saudi support:** KSA VAT return reconciled against the ledger; checks before submit for what ZATCA e-invoicing would reject; GOSI, Mudad payroll file and end-of-service under the Saudi Labor Law.
- **University:** study plans and prerequisites, registration with sections, seat limits and timetable clash checks, student self-service registration, GPA and academic standing, degree audit, verifiable transcripts.
- **Maintenance Center:** health checks that say what is wrong and what to do, and safe one-click repairs with a backup first.
- **Quality:** every release passes an automated suite of over 130 tests on a fresh install.

Frappe and ERPNext are open-source projects by Frappe Technologies; SPEED-DO extends them.
